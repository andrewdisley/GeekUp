GeekUp is a grass-roots knowledge sharing and networking social for folks involved or interested in all forms of technology and creative media.

This event is part GeekUp reunion, open to any previous GeekUp attendees, and part open call for new people interested in what GeekUp could offer them.

We'll be running a one off session at Leigh Hackspace. This is a purely informal session, no talks will be run, but people are invitied to bring projects to demonstrate to other members.

We'll be hosting in our bar, a range of pizza will be provided and there's a stocked tuck shop on site with soft drinks and snacks. You're welcome to bring your own alcoholic beverages.

Grab a free ticket if you want to come along https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/geekup-leigh-tickets-1754956715779

Parking is available on-site. full details for getting to the venue at https://www.leighhack.org/visit/.
